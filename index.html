About

How I work

Most of my professional work has been around business-critical systems where a small code change can affect orders, payments, customer service workflows or operational teams. That has shaped the way I approach software.

I like understanding the business rules first then deciding where complexity belongs. I prefer designs that are easy to explain, test and support rather than architecture that exists to look impressive on a diagram.

I am currently going deeper into distributed systems, domain-driven design, cloud architecture and AI engineering while continuing to build on a long background in .NET systems.

01
Start with the problem

Technology should earn its place by solving something real.

02
Design for failure

Retries, duplicates and partial failures are part of the system.

03
Make behaviour visible

Logs, traces and metrics should help answer useful questions.

04
Keep change safe

Good boundaries and tests should make the next change easier.
